Mirrorboard

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Mirrorboard is a Mac app that copies the home screen layout of an iPhone onto an iPad. It’s built to keep your information on your Mac. This policy explains the little that leaves it.

What stays on your Mac

Mirrorboard reads your devices’ home screen layouts, app lists and app icons directly over the cable or Wi‑Fi connection between your Mac and your devices. It keeps layouts it has read, backups taken before every change, and app icons on your Mac only, in your user Library folder. None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Delete the app’s folders in ~/Library/Application Support/Mirrorboard and ~/Library/Caches/Mirrorboard to remove them.

What Mirrorboard sends

  • Your licence key, when you unlock Mirrorboard and when it opens, to Lemon Squeezy, which sells Mirrorboard on our behalf, to check that the key is valid. It’s sent with the label “Mac”; nothing about you or your devices goes with it.
  • Update checks, to GitHub, which hosts Mirrorboard’s update feed. Like any website, GitHub can see the network address the request comes from.

Mirrorboard has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

When you buy

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. They collect what’s needed to take payment and issue your receipt and licence key, under their privacy policy. We can see your name, email address and order details in our Lemon Squeezy account, and use them only to provide support and refunds.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ network addresses. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. To show the latest version, the page asks GitHub which release is current.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted, by emailing mirrorboard.app@gmail.com. If you’re in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
