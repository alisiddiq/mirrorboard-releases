14 days, no questions asked

If Mirrorboard isn’t right for you, you can have a full refund within 14 days of buying it, for any reason.

How to ask

Email mirrorboard.app@gmail.com with the email address you bought with, or reply to your receipt. You’ll usually hear back within two working days.

What happens next

The refund goes back to the way you paid, through Lemon Squeezy, our reseller. It usually reaches you within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank. Your licence key stops working, and Mirrorboard goes back to the free preview.

After 14 days

If something isn’t working, please get in touch anyway: we’ll try to fix it, and refund you if we can’t. This doesn’t affect your rights under consumer law.
